## Evidence and findings

The public Nominatim policy caps normal use at one request per second per application, requires a valid identifying user agent/referrer, attribution and caching. Bulk geocoding is discouraged and long-running recurring scripts face stricter constraints. The policy recommends a commercial provider or self-hosted Nominatim for larger production needs.

- Usage policy: https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/
- OpenStreetMap copyright/licence: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

## Decision detail

Do not make FlyttSignal operationally dependent on the public community instance. A separately assessed self-hosted instance or contracted provider could receive a new `GO` assessment. The existing MapLibre basemap remains a different use case and is not changed by this review.
