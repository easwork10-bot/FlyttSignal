# HSB Uppsala — direct public rental search

Reviewed: 2026-08-29

## Decision

`CONTROLLED_LIVE_DEVELOPMENT`. The direct channel is complete only for its explicitly proven
Uppsala-municipality scope; HSB inventory distributed through HomeQ remains a separate channel.

## Evidence

HSB Uppsala states that its available rentals are shown under `Sök bostad`, while some HSB Uppsala rentals are published only on HomeQ. The county search exposed five current rentals during review, three of them in Uppsala municipality. The direct Uppsala objects were:

| Stable HSB path | Address | Rooms | Area | Rent | Move-in |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `1801-loten-43/9091` | Ferlinsgatan 15 B | 2 | 43.3 m² | 5,341 SEK | 2026-12-01 |
| `1801-loten-43/9115` | Ferlinsgatan 23 B | 2 | 42.5 m² | 5,326 SEK | 2026-12-01 |
| `1845-falhagen-236/50` | Torkelsgatan 11 | 1 | 36.0 m² | 7,078 SEK | 2026-12-01 |

The Torkelsgatan detail page also exposed unit number `50` and application deadline `2026-09-03`. The fixture stores only these non-personal listing facts. It excludes descriptions, images, contact details, application forms and applicant data.

## Channel model

- `hsb-direct`: HSB is both publisher and upstream landlord. The stable object identity is the property/listing path, not only the street address.
- `homeq-public-search`: a separate, partial marketplace route for HSB objects published on HomeQ.
- Cross-channel matching may use upstream provider, normalized address, unit identifier, rent, area and availability, but one channel must never silently overwrite the other's provenance.
- The deterministic fixture remains parser evidence. A separate live adapter reads the server-rendered public cards, rejects every municipality path except Uppsala/Uppsala, caps results at 50 and stores only identity, address, rooms, area, rent, dates, contract badge and HSB provenance.
- Rule `hsb-public-html-v2` cross-checks county listing cards against the public property-card
  availability totals, then repeats that comparison for the filtered Uppsala municipality path.
  Missing counts, count mismatches, pagination controls and the configured cap fail closed.
- On 2026-08-29 the page exposed five county listings and property-card availability also summed to
  five. Two listing cards were in Uppsala municipality and its property-card availability summed to
  two. A controlled persisted run therefore recorded 2 of 2 and `COMPLETE` for this direct scope.
- This does not claim completeness for HSB homes published only through HomeQ. Missing objects still
  cannot create removal events in M3.1. Three consecutive scheduled v2 `COMPLETE` runs are required
  before any HSB lifecycle can be considered.
