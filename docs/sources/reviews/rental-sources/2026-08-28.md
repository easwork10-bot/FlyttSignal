# Uppsala rental-source selection

Reviewed: 2026-08-28

## Inventory basis

Hyresgästföreningen maintains a municipality-indexed directory of major public and private landlords. Its Uppsala page currently lists Uppsalahem, Nationsgårdarna, ByggVesta, Heimstaden, Ikano Bostad, HSB, Newsec, Stena Fastigheter, Rikshem, Riksbyggen, Victoriahem and Balder.

Use this directory as a **coverage-audit and source-discovery input**, not as a rental-listing signal. It identifies landlords but does not establish current ownership, current vacancies or the channel used for each vacancy. Every distribution claim below was therefore checked against the landlord's own current material.

- Directory: https://www.hyresgastforeningen.se/om-oss/hyresvardar/hyresvardar/
- Uppsala inventory: https://www.hyresgastforeningen.se/om-oss/hyresvardar/hyresvardar-i-uppsala/

## Decision

1. Build Uppsala Bostadsförmedling fixture-first as the first M2.3 rental adapter.
2. Do not build separate Uppsalahem or Rikshem adapters because their Uppsala availability is upstreamed through the selected source.
3. Review Heimstaden next as a complementary direct source because its own Uppsala inventory is only partly mediated through the selected source.
4. Keep HomeQ as the preferred future partner/API route, not an anonymous public API. It covers Balder, Riksbyggen, Ikano Bostad and part of HSB's distribution, but its documented inventory and listing endpoints are landlord-scoped and require landlord-portal credentials and onboarding.
5. ByggVesta's own queue is reviewed and partner-gated: current vacancies require applicant login and the legacy public market hostname no longer resolves.
6. Keep Nationsgårdarna fixture-only until a legitimate authenticated or partner interface is available; current vacancies require login.

## Exact Heimstaden comparison

On 2026-08-28, Heimstaden's public Uppsala page showed three available objects on Kryddblandargatan. The Uppsala Bostadsförmedling filter `landlord=Heimstaden` showed six different objects on Sernanders väg. This proves current partial—not complete—overlap. Both channels must retain `Heimstaden` as the same upstream provider and must not receive an independent-source bonus.
